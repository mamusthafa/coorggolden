Additional Charges & Policies

Important information about our pricing and policies

  • Waiting Charges: Complimentary waiting time of 60 minutes for airport pickups and 30 minutes for local bookings. Beyond that, ₹200 per hour.
  • Night Charges: 25% extra applicable between 10 PM to 6 AM.
  • Toll & Taxes: All toll charges, state taxes, and driver allowances are included in our quoted fares.
  • Cancellation Policy: Free cancellation up to 6 hours before scheduled pickup. 50% charge for cancellations within 6 hours.
  • Extra Kilometer: ₹15 per km for sedan, ₹20 per km for SUV, ₹25 per km for Innova Crysta beyond the agreed route.
  • Bridge & Road Taxes: Any special bridge or road taxes applicable on specific routes are included in the fare.

Frequently Asked Questions - Taxi Fares

Are your fares fixed or do they change based on traffic?

Our fares are fixed based on the route and vehicle type, not on actual travel time or traffic conditions. You pay the agreed amount regardless of delays due to traffic, weather, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Do you offer discounts for round-trip bookings?

Yes, we offer attractive discounts for round-trip bookings. Typically, you can save 10-15% compared to booking two separate one-way journeys. Please mention your return trip requirement when booking for the best price.

Are there any hidden charges in your pricing?

Absolutely not! We believe in transparent pricing. Our quoted fares include all toll charges, state taxes, driver allowances, and basic vehicle maintenance. The only potential extras are waiting charges beyond complimentary time, night charges (10 PM-6 AM), and extra kilometers beyond the agreed route.

How far in advance should I book to get the best rates?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for the best vehicle availability and pricing. However, we do accommodate last-minute bookings based on availability. For peak tourist seasons (October-March), earlier booking is advisable.
