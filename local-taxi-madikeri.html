Frequently Asked Questions - Local Taxi Service

Do you provide airport transfers within Coorg?

Yes, we provide pick-up and drop-off services to the Madikeri helipad and other local landing points within Coorg. For the main airports (Bangalore/Mangalore), please refer to our airport taxi service.

Can I book a taxi for just a few hours?

Absolutely! We offer hourly hire services starting from a minimum of 2 hours. This is perfect for short trips, shopping, or visiting a couple of nearby attractions.

Are your drivers familiar with offbeat locations in Coorg?

Yes, our drivers are locals or have extensive experience in the region. They know not only the popular tourist spots but also hidden gems, viewpoints, coffee plantations, and lesser-known temples that most tourists miss.

Do you provide child seats for local travel?

We can provide child seats upon request. Please inform us at the time of booking about the child's age and weight so we can arrange the appropriate seat. We recommend making this request at least 24 hours in advance.
