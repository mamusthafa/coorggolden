Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 15-30 minutes via WhatsApp and within 2 hours for emails during business hours (8 AM - 8 PM). For urgent requests, we recommend contacting us directly via WhatsApp or phone.

What information do you need for a booking?

To provide you with an accurate quote, we typically need: pickup location, drop location, date, preferred time, number of passengers, vehicle preference (if any), and any special requirements like child seats or extra luggage.

Do you require advance payment for bookings?

For most bookings, payment is made directly to the driver at the end of the journey. For advance bookings or special requests, we may request a small advance amount via secure online transfer or UPI, which will be discussed during booking confirmation.

Can I make changes to my booking after confirmation?

Yes, we understand that plans can change. You can modify your booking subject to availability. We recommend contacting us as soon as possible for any changes. Changes made less than 6 hours before the scheduled pickup may be subject to availability and potential charges.
