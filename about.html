About Coorg Golden Holidays

Your trusted partner for reliable and comfortable taxi services in Coorg

Our Journey

Founded with a vision to provide reliable and comfortable transportation in the beautiful hill station of Coorg (Kodagu), Coorg Golden Holidays has grown to become one of the most trusted taxi services in the region. What started as a small initiative to serve tourists and locals alike has evolved into a professional organization dedicated to excellence in service.

Our Mission

To provide safe, comfortable, and affordable transportation solutions that enhance the travel experience of our customers while showcasing the natural beauty and hospitality of Coorg.

Our Vision

To be the preferred choice for taxi services in Coorg, known for reliability, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Why Choose Us?

Years of Experience

With years of serving both domestic and international tourists, we understand the unique needs of travelers to Coorg.

Professional Drivers

Our drivers are trained, courteed, and possess extensive knowledge of Coorg's roads and attractions.

Well-Maintained Fleet
